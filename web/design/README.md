# T01 design references

Built-in imagegen was used with the imagegen skill; no CLI/API-key fallback was used. `t01-dashboard-reference.png` is the first concept, superseded after the user requested a separate Settings page. `t01-job-board-reference.png` is the accepted direction: prominent search, job cards beside selected details, compact progress, and Settings in navigation. The working implementation also incorporates the user's later Applied-tab request. Generated imagery is a design reference, not a screenshot used as application UI, and none of these images is shipped in `build/`.

The final generation prompt was:

> Create a refined realistic flat browser UI screenshot for 'Internship Pipeline', a self-hosted internship job board. Landscape 1536x1024. The user requests a main page inspired by the focused familiar job-search experience of LinkedIn, Indeed and modern clean recruiting boards. The entire image must be ONLY the jobs page. SETTINGS MUST BE A SEPARATE PAGE accessed through a top nav link, never embedded or previewed at bottom. Horizontal white top navigation with a small indigo IP monogram and Internship Pipeline name at left, Jobs selected and Settings nav links at right. Light warm-gray canvas, dark navy text, restrained indigo primary accent. A prominent search hero near top saying 'Find your next chapter.' below small uppercase YOUR INTERNSHIP SEARCH. Wide bordered search field 'Role, company, or keyword' beside a location field and purple Search button. Below search is a compact filters row, '10,000 opportunities', sort dropdown 'Date posted' and direction control. Main area uses two columns: left about 42% stacked white job cards with selected indigo border, fictional companies Northstar Labs, Cedar Analytics, Harbor Robotics, scannable role title, company/location, Posted date and separate First observed label, match score pill, eligibility badge, save bookmark. Right about 58% white selected job detail panel with sticky header title Data Science Intern, company Northstar Labs, San Francisco CA, score 92, explicit eligible badge, Apply status control 'Mark as applied' and secondary Save button. Detail has short readable About this role and Requirements sections, Posted Oct 4 2026, First observed Oct 6 2026, Deadline Oct 25 2026. Above two columns place an understated one-line running search status 'Collecting 48 of 120 sources' with narrow indigo progress bar and Pause control. At bottom of left list pagination shows Previous, Page 1 of 400, Next. Small top 'Synthetic preview' badge. No employer logos or illustrations, no photos, no gradients, no inset settings menu, no settings form anywhere. Prioritize implementation-ready visual hierarchy, comfortable whitespace, high contrast readable sans serif type.

Browser screenshots `t01-desktop-jobs.png`, `t01-desktop-settings.png`, `t01-mobile-jobs.png`, and `t01-mobile-settings.png` depict the implemented UI, not imagegen output. The implementation deliberately uses one combined role/company/location search and working status filters rather than adding nonfunctional filters from the concept. No particular product named Runway was assumed.

## Flat refinement — October 6, 2026

`flat-jobs-reference.png` and `flat-settings-reference.png` are the built-in ImageGen directions used for the flat refinement. They guide white continuous surfaces, divider-based rows, underlined tabs, unboxed settings, and restrained indigo. The app retains its supported data fields and workflows rather than reproducing invented mock content. Verification and screenshot paths are recorded in `docs/design-qa.md`. These images are design references and are not shipped in application assets.
